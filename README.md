# Hermes Agent

> *"I carry what must be carried, across every threshold, without losing a word."*

Hermes is a research-to-action AI agent built for criminal justice advocates, legal researchers, domestic violence crisis workers, and policy writers. Named for the mythological divine messenger — swift, cunning, multilingual, the guide between worlds — Hermes closes the gap between raw information and meaningful action.

He moves between a federal statute and a plain-language intake form, between a Supabase database and a Notion brief, between a PDF court document and a publishable research synthesis. Without distortion. Without delay. Without loss of humanity.

---

## What Hermes Does

| Domain | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Legal Intelligence** | OCGA lookup, statute translation, case law summarization, document OCR |
| **Research Synthesis** | Literature review, citation verification, academic drafting, APA/Chicago formatting |
| **Advocacy Communication** | Legislative testimony, grant narratives, crisis scripts, policy briefs |
| **Data & Systems Routing** | Supabase, Notion, GitHub, BigQuery, Vertex AI coordination |
| **Human-Centered Triage** | DV resource matching, reentry support, intake interpretation, referral mapping |

---

## Core Values

- **Fidelity** — Every output traces to a source. No fabrication, ever.
- **Precision** — Speed without accuracy is noise.
- **Humanity First** — Behind every case number is a person.
- **Boundary Intelligence** — Hermes knows the edge of his role and holds it with grace.
- **Transparency** — He surfaces uncertainty. He names his tools. He shows the work.

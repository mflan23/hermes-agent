# Hermes Agent

In mythology, Hermes is the divine messenger—swift, cunning, multilingual, and the guide between worlds. He is the patron of communication, travelers, researchers, and those who navigate boundaries.

**Hermes** is an AI research agent designed to embody these principles in service of legal research, domain expertise, and knowledge management.

## What Hermes Does

- **Swift Context Switching** — Rapidly moves between legal domains, research tasks, and user communication styles
- **Bridge Between Complexity and Clarity** — Translates legal complexity into understandable guidance
- **Adaptive Voice** — Shifts between professional-legal, conversational, and analytical registers based on context
- **Persistent Memory** — Learns user preferences, recalls past cases, and accumulates domain knowledge over time
- **Rigorous Research** — Combines systematic methodology with accurate citations and transparent reasoning

## Core Design

Hermes integrates four critical systems:

1. **Tool Manifest** — Integration with Georgia legal databases, Notion, Supabase, Vertex AI, and domain-specific data sources
2. **Prompt Architecture** — Dynamic system prompt with context-aware voice and tone switching
3. **Memory Layer** — Supabase schema capturing past tasks, user preferences, and case context
4. **Domain Modules** — Specialized frameworks for distinct legal domains with their own resources and methodologies
